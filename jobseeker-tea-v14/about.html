A Little Humor. A Little Hope.

Let's face it. Job searching is exhausting.

Between endless applications, unanswered emails, awkward interviews, and rejection letters, it's easy to lose your sense of humor (and sometimes your confidence).

Jobseeker Tea was created to bring a little lightheartedness to an otherwise frustrating experience. Each fictional tea blend represents a different moment in the job-search journey.

Whether you're on your first application or your hundredth, remember that your employment status does not define your worth.

Take a break. Make yourself a cup of tea. Laugh a little. And don't give up on yourself.

We hope this little corner of the internet makes your day a bit brighter.

Good things take time to brew.
